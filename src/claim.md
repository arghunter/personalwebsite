# Why?

A few years ago, I learned how suddenly life could end. I suppose I always knew in theory, but experience is a bit harsher. 

I realized that I didn't want to live life wondering what could've been. I'd rather fail infinitely chasing impossible dreams than play it safe for eternity. 

If I'm being honest, I consider myself a failure. Not in the doom and despair sense that I'll never achieve anything, but in that satistically most things I try to do fail. Right before writing this I failed to solder an IMU; It took me two weeks of straight failures to successfully read and write from DDR3; It took me over 200 tries to sucessfully read data from a singular PDM microphone. Frankly, I wouldn't have it any other way. A life without failure is monotonus

I try to live each day like it could be my last. Not that it is my last, but that if it were all end today, I could at rest easy knowing that I gave my all. 

<div class="accent">
I live each day with 3 goals.

1. To live as me to the fullest
2. To lift up my friends
3. To keep building cool things
