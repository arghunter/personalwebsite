## Why?
I've had a personal website since 10th grade. I always wanted to have a personal space to showcase what I work on, however I personally hate website development, so my sites have always been scuffed and buggy. But then, my friend [Liam Hanrahan](https://www.outercloud.dev/) built his own website, and it looked really good. So naturally, I had to improve mine.

## What Did I Use?
This website is directly forked from Liam Hanrahan's website, with a few improvements of my own. I primarily do hardware and low-level software. Web dev is far from my favorite task, so naturally, I asked his permission and based my website off of his. Initially I decided to use AI for styling and some widgets. Eventually, though I realized that I'd rather style it myself even if I personally hate CSS with a burning passion. Admittedly, some parts are still by AI due to it touching most files, but the website is gradually becoming more me.
